# Expressive voice-overs

- **A · Engine inspector.** Choose Standard or Expressive above the voice list. In Script, open **Insert tag** and pick a pause, mood, sound or voice at the cursor.
- **B · Script mode.** Switch engines beside the draft title. Type `[` in the script, narrow the list by typing, then press Enter to insert the first match. **Insert tag** opens the full menu.
- **C · Voice library.** Switch engines under **Voice source** in the voice library. Select a passage, open **Insert tag**, and choose a voice or mood to wrap those words.

**Recommendation.** Start with A. Staff can see the engine, language and available voices together, and the script needs just one Insert control. All three use the same picker, including keyboard filtering.

**Trying the prototypes.** Each starts in Standard. The first Expressive download can be cancelled or allowed to finish for the Arabic sample with Maha. Choose English for the full sound list. Create adds the result to Your voice-overs, where each row plays or pauses its preview. Downloads, recorded voices, generation and playback timing are simulated in memory. Reload to reset.
